How I Work

My work usually starts with a messy question:

  • What are we really trying to understand?
  • What decision will this support?
  • What data can we trust?
  • What should be automated, modeled, generated, or left human-in-the-loop?
  • How will we know if the system is working?

From there, I move from ambiguity to architecture: define the problem, shape the data, build the prototype, evaluate the output, harden the system, and create the documentation or governance needed for others to trust and use it.

I am comfortable operating across strategy and implementation: executive communication, product framing, data pipelines, model evaluation, AI architecture, experimentation, stakeholder alignment, and team enablement.

HitsHome — AI Decision Intelligence for Real Estate

Project type: Founder project / AI platform / real estate intelligence Role: Founder & Technical Lead Status: In development

HitsHome is an AI-powered decision intelligence platform for real estate and location analytics. It is designed to help homebuyers, investors, and decision-makers evaluate markets, properties, risks, and tradeoffs using structured data, explainable scoring, and AI-assisted workflows.

What it includes

  • Multi-source geographic data pipelines across Census, housing, economic, and market datasets.
  • Standardized features across counties, places/cities, ZIP/ZCTA areas, and states.
  • Explainable scoring for market attractiveness, investment potential, risk, and decision confidence.
  • Personalized market matching and property matching concepts.
  • Agent-enabled workflows for structured research, comparison, and decision support.
  • Governance concepts around source quality, stale-data windows, confidence scoring, and explainability.

Why it matters

Real estate decisions are emotionally and financially significant, yet the available information is fragmented, overly generic, or hard to interpret. HitsHome is an attempt to make complex location and property decisions clearer, more personal, and more evidence-based.
